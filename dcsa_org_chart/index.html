User Manual

This tool builds a DCSA ready Legal Organization Chart and supporting package for a Facility Clearance (FCL) submission. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Your work is saved automatically in this browser.

The fastest path (5 minutes)

1
Load a sample. Click Simple Sample (single owner) or Complex Sample (tiered ownership with foreign minority) at the top right. This fills every field so you can see a complete, valid package and then edit it.
2
Add your logo and website. Go to Branding, upload a logo and type your real website. These appear on the chart header and every export.
3
Fill the Company Profile. Legal name, business type, CAGE Code, UEI, FCL status, and your SMO / FSO / ITPSO. Fields marked * are required by DCSA.
4
Build the ownership chain. In Entity Builder add every company in the chain (parent companies, intermediate parents, the ultimate parent, subsidiaries, affiliates) and each owning person. Set the Parent Entity field to link them, and fill ownership % and voting %.
5
List Key Management Personnel. In KMP Builder add officers and directors with titles and citizenship.
6
Review the Dashboard. Fix any red or yellow warnings. Aim for a readiness score of 100%.
7
Generate and export. Open Chart Generator to view the chart, then Exports to download the editable PowerPoint, PDF, PNG, Word comments, and Excel data.

How the chart is built (important)

  • Every row in Entity Builder is a node on the chart. A node is drawn as a rectangle if it is a company and an oval if it is a person.
  • The Parent Entity field controls who sits above whom. Type the exact name of the parent into the child's Parent Entity field. The ultimate parent (no parent) is drawn at the top; the cleared facility sits at the bottom of its chain.
  • A person is detected automatically when the name has no company words (Inc, LLC, Corp, etc.) or the Business Type is set to U.S. Person / Individual. You can force it by choosing the U.S. Person business type.
  • Connector lines carry the ownership % (solid line) and voting / control % (dashed line) per the DCSA legend, which is printed on every chart.
  • Foreign entities and foreign persons are outlined in red; U.S. ones in black.

The editable PowerPoint

The PowerPoint export creates native, editable shapes - real rectangles, ovals, lines, and text boxes - not a picture. After downloading you can open it in PowerPoint, click any box, move it, retype the name, or change a percentage. The same coordinates are used for the PDF and PNG so all three match.

DCSA requirements this tool covers

  • All U.S. and foreign parent companies, subsidiaries, and affiliates.
  • The association between the cleared facility and the ultimate parent.
  • Percentages of ownership and voting, FCL status, and CAGE codes for each entity.
  • Key Management Personnel with titles and citizenship.
  • A FOCI summary and an auto generated DCSA package comments narrative.
  • A legend, cover information, and supporting tables in the Output Packet.

Tips

  • Click Save (top right) to download a JSON backup. Load restores it later or on another computer.
  • Ownership percentages at the same level should total 100%. The Dashboard warns you if they do not.
  • If a node shows the wrong shape, set its Business Type explicitly (Corporation/LLC for a box, U.S. Person for an oval).
