Help & Instructions

How to use this tool

  1. Run the Setup Wizard. Enter your company details, security roles, facility information, and logo. Everything saves automatically in this browser.
  2. Watch your readiness score. It reaches 100% when every required field is complete.
  3. Generate documents from the Dashboard. Preview first, then Download Word. Use "Full Binder" to get everything as one zip.
  4. Open in Word and finish. Review each document, adjust anything specific to your environment, and have your SMO sign the approval block.
  5. Keep evidence. Use the forms, logs, and checklists day to day; they are the records an assessor will ask to see. The Evidence Checklist card tells you exactly what to keep.

Word, PDF, and CSV

"Download Word" saves a .doc file that opens in Microsoft Word (and LibreOffice). For a PDF, open the Preview and click "Print / Save as PDF". The control matrix also exports as CSV for Excel.

Glossary

Compliance alignment

All generated documents are aligned with applicable CMMC and DFARS requirements and carry a compliance references section covering 32 CFR Part 170 (CMMC Program Rule), CMMC Level 2, NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 (all 110 requirements), DFARS 252.204-7012, -7019, -7020, and -7021, DoD CUI Registry requirements, and any applicable contract, DD Form 254, SOW, security classification guide, or CUI handling instruction.
