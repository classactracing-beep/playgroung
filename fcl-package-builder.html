Business Structure

Your legal business structure determines which organizational documents DCSA requires in your FCL package. Select one:

KMP — Key Management Personnel

Who counts as KMP? The President/CEO, the SMO, the FSO, the ITPSO, and other officers, directors, partners, or officials who can affect the organization's policies or performance on classified contracts. KMP must generally be U.S. citizens; foreign nationals in KMP positions raise FOCI concerns that require mitigation. Non-essential KMP may be formally excluded by board resolution.

Excluded Tier Entity

What is an excluded tier entity? When a parent company in the ownership chain does not need access to classified information, it can be formally excluded from the FCL by board resolutions, so the parent does not need its own clearance. The exclusion resolution must state that the excluded entity will not be given access to classified information and will not influence classified performance. FOCI in the chain may still require mitigation (SSA, Proxy Agreement, Voting Trust, Board Resolution).

Required Training (STEPP)

The FSO must complete FSO program management training within 6 months of appointment. The ITPSO must complete insider threat training. Courses are free via CDSE's STEPP portal (cdse.usalearning.gov).
