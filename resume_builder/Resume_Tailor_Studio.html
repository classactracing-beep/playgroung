9User Guide

Everything a first time user needs to know.

1. How to paste or upload a resume
  1. Click the upload box in step 1 and pick a .txt or .docx file, or drag the file onto the box.
  2. Or simply paste your resume into the text area.
  3. Keep it complete and honest: real titles, employers, dates, schools, and certifications. The tool can only tailor what you give it.
2. How to paste a job posting
Open the posting, select all of it (title, duties, requirements, preferred qualifications), copy it, and paste into step 2. The more complete the posting, the better the keyword extraction.
3. How to use a job posting link
You can record the link in step 2 for your own reference, but browsers do not allow a web page to read other websites (a security rule called CORS). That means this tool cannot scrape the link for you. Open the link yourself, copy the posting text, and paste it in.
4. How keyword matching works
  • The tool scans the posting for skills, tools, duties, and qualifications using a built in dictionary plus phrase analysis.
  • Each keyword is checked against your resume text.
  • Matched keywords exist in both, so they are emphasized in your tailored resume.
  • Missing keywords are shown only as a heads up. They are never added automatically, because you should not claim skills you do not have. If you really do have one, mention it in your resume text and analyze again.
5. How to review the tailored resume
After you click Generate Tailored Resume, read every section in step 5 and the preview in step 7. Confirm that all dates, employers, numbers, and claims are accurate. The tool rewrites wording, but you own the facts.
6. How to edit before export
Every section in the builder is a normal text box. Edit anything: summary, competencies, bullets, skills, education, certifications, clearance, and contractor details. The preview and the exports always use your latest edits. In the experience box, lines starting with a dash become bullets and other lines become bold job headers.
7. How to download Word and PDF files
Use the Export Center. Download Word Document creates a clean .docx with simple headings and no tables, which applicant tracking systems read easily. Download PDF creates a text based PDF (real selectable text, not an image). Download Full Package bundles everything in one zip.
8. How to copy the hiring manager email
Generate the email in step 6, edit it so it sounds like you, then click Copy Email in the Export Center. Paste it into your email client, attach the resume, double check the recipient, and send.
9. What this tool can and cannot do
  • Can: extract job posting keywords, measure your match, rewrite your real experience in a chosen tone and format, build a clean ATS friendly resume, draft a short email, and export Word, PDF, and text files. Everything runs locally in your browser.
  • Cannot: scrape job links, invent experience or credentials, guarantee interviews, or replace your judgment. It also cannot read .pdf resumes; save those as .docx or .txt first, or paste the text.
10. Final reminder: verify before you send
Before submitting anything, verify every date, employer, title, metric, certification, and clearance on the finished resume and in the email. You are responsible for the accuracy of every application you submit. When in doubt, soften or remove the claim.
